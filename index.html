Before you start

Stayingfit keeps what you log (weight, meals, meal photos, water and workouts) so Pushpanjali and her team can guide your plan.

  • Only Pushpanjali and her assistant can see your logs and photos. Other clients see only your first name and progress on the leaderboard.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with anyone else.
  • You can ask Pushpanjali to delete your account and data at any time.
