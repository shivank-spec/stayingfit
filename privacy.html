Terms and privacy notice

Stayingfit · last updated 14 November 2026

Stayingfit is Pushpanjali Khaitan's app for her nutrition coaching clients. By using it, you agree to the terms below and to your information being handled as described here.

Part 1 · Terms of use

What the app is for

Logging your weight, water, meals, meal photos and workouts, seeing your diet plan, and messaging Pushpanjali and her assistant. It supports your coaching plan; it doesn't replace it.

Not medical advice

Nothing in the app is a medical diagnosis or treatment. Calorie numbers are estimates for typical home servings. The colours shown next to blood test results are a general guide only. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or feel unwell, speak to your doctor, and in an emergency contact emergency services, not the app.

Your account

  • Your login is for you alone. Choose your own password and don't share it.
  • If you forget your password, ask Pushpanjali to reset it.
  • Log honestly. Pushpanjali plans your diet from what you enter.
  • Be respectful in chats and family groups.

Your plan

The app doesn't take payments. Your plan, its dates and its renewal are arranged directly with Pushpanjali. Access to the app may end when your plan ends.

Part 2 · Privacy notice

What we collect

  • Your name, a username and a password. The password is stored only in scrambled form, so nobody can read it.
  • What you choose to log: body weight, meals and calories, meal photos, water, workouts, the liquids you tick off, and your messages.
  • Only if you separately agree: blood test results (HbA1c, cholesterol, triglycerides) and report files.
  • Only if you turn on notifications: the address your phone or browser gives the app so that it can send them to you.
  • What Pushpanjali and her assistant write for your coaching: your diets, comments on your meal photos, and short private notes about your plan (for example your food preferences).

Why

Only to run your coaching: to plan your diet, track your progress, tell you about new messages, remind you about check-ins and renewals, and show your stars, badges and leaderboard position.

Who can see it

  • Pushpanjali and her assistant can see your logs, photos, messages and, if you agreed, blood test results.
  • Other clients see only your first name, the first letter of your surname and your progress numbers on the leaderboard. They never see your weight in kg, your photos, your messages or your health results.
  • Members of a family group can read the messages in that group.
  • Notifications travel through your phone or browser maker's notification service (Google, Apple or Mozilla). The text is encrypted on the way, so those companies cannot read it.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

Where it's stored

With Supabase, a database and file storage provider, on servers in South Korea (Seoul). Access rules in the database mean each login can only reach what it is allowed to. Meal photos and report files are private. The app itself keeps a few small settings on your device, such as whether sounds are on and which messages you have already read.

How long we keep it

While you are a client. Your account and everything you logged are deleted within 90 days after your plan ends or after you ask, unless you ask Pushpanjali to keep them.

Your choices

You can ask Pushpanjali at any time to correct your information, give you a copy of it (including the notes kept about your coaching), or delete your account and everything you logged. You can stop sharing blood test results by asking her to remove them. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings, and signing out stops them on that device.

Questions

Message Pushpanjali in the app's chat, or contact her the way you usually do.
